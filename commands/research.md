# /research

Research the question in `$ARGUMENTS`. Prefer primary and authoritative sources,
record links and dates, distinguish facts from inference, and keep quotations
short. When invoked inside a Spec Kit feature, write findings to that feature's
`research.md`; otherwise return a concise cited report. Do not invoke a separate
orchestration daemon or hidden memory store.
